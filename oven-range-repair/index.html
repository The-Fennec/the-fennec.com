Common problems

Sound familiar? We fix these every day.

🔥

Oven Won't Heat

You preheat and it stays cold. A worn igniter or burned-out bake element is the most common cause.

Most common oven repair
⚡

Burner Clicks but Won't Light

Constant clicking with no flame means a worn igniter, bad spark module or clogged burner ports.

Safety risk if ignored
🌡️

Wrong Temperature

Set it to 375°F and it bakes like 300°F. A failing temperature sensor or thermostat throws off every recipe.

Ruins meals without you noticing
💡

Error Codes or Dead Controls

Beeping, unresponsive buttons or flashing codes. We diagnose control boards and touchpads on all major brands.

Can get worse quickly
🚪

Door Won't Seal

A loose hinge or worn gasket leaks heat, so food cooks unevenly and the oven works harder.

Wastes energy every use
