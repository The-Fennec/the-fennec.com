Common problems

Sound familiar? We fix these every day.

🛑

Won't Drain

Standing water in the bottom means a clogged filter, drain hose or a failed drain pump.

Breeds bacteria fast
🚿

Dishes Still Dirty

Clogged spray arms, a weak wash pump or a bad inlet valve. We fix the cause, not just clean it.

Often a quick fix
💧

Leaking Under the Door

Worn door gasket, pump seal or loose connection. We find the source and stop the leak.

Protect your floors now
📟

Won't Start or Controls Dead

No lights, no cycle, or error codes. Usually a door latch, control board or power issue.

Often repairable same day
🔇

Grinding or Humming

Something may be stuck in the pump or the motor is failing. We open it up, clear it and test a full cycle.

Can damage the pump
