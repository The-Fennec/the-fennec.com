Common problems

Sound familiar? We fix these every day.

🧊

Fridge Not Cooling

Warm milk, soft butter, a freezer that's barely cold. Usually a failing fan, dirty condenser, defrost problem or start relay.

Act fast — food spoils within 4 hours
💧

Water Leaking on the Floor

A clogged defrost drain, cracked water line or bad door seal. We find the source and fix it right.

Can damage flooring over time
🔊

Loud or Strange Noises

Clicking, buzzing, rattling or humming. Most of the time it's a fan motor or compressor relay asking for help.

Noise = stress on the system
🧊

Ice Maker Not Working

Frozen fill tube, bad water inlet valve or a failed ice maker assembly. Usually a quick fix.

Often fixed same day
❄️

Freezer Too Warm or Frosting Up

Frost buildup or a freezer that won't hold temperature points to the defrost system or thermostat.

Saves hundreds in wasted food
